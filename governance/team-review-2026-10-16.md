## Overall assessment
Real work, code, logs and specialist runs exist. It is false to describe everything as simulation or three days without contact. It is equally false to call the team reliably effective merely because runs succeeded. The failures include stale and fragmented evidence, unsupported progress/attribution claims, late architectural challenge, specification expansion before decisive product proof, and no dependable Cadence intake/follow-up. Product browser acceptance remained distinct from component checks.

No defensible total efficiency or cost comparison with Peter plus one LLM can be calculated from the available fragmented records. Isolated run durations/costs are not total project cost. Future comparisons need comparable scope, accepted outcomes, actual elapsed time, reported usage/cost, rework and defects, with customer judgment of product value retained separately. No claim of savings or superior quality is justified yet.

**Failure:** status answers were repeatedly given without sufficient current primary-source inspection. Historical and current connectivity were conflated. The plan expanded beyond a small Cadence role, and architectural feasibility and final user-visible proof were sought too late. **Correction:** revised Atlas skill, startup snapshot, governance baseline and routing profiles. Inspect current task-relevant originals before status; report inspected scope and unknowns; consult Forge before material technical commitment; prove the critical real path early; record dissent and missing contributions; route triggered Cadence work; distinguish author/executor/publisher/tester; correct affected records. No invented cross-chat availability or Claude activity.

**Proof still needed:** a fresh task where Atlas reconstructs current state correctly, obtains the real relevant specialists, discloses blockers and closes only the proven outcome. Updated prose alone does not prove this behavior.
