# Agentarium Process 1.2
Companion to `agentarium-rulebook-v1.2.md`; same adoption/authority limits. Applies to task routing, not a mandatory ceremony.

1. **Reconstruct:** Atlas checks current task, baseline, original handbacks and outstanding decisions. State unknowns; reconcile duplicate/stale records.
2. **Frame:** Record outcome and applicable Epic authority. Use Nova for material vision work and Scout for consequential factual uncertainty. A small fix may need neither a new Epic nor a full spec.
3. **Prove the risk:** Forge advises architecture/feasibility. Before costly expansion, exercise the smallest authorized real end-to-end path, or report its blocker and consequence. Archibald specifies only the necessary detail.
4. **Contribute:** Invite relevant team judgment. For a material team-wide change seek all members' views, preserve dissent and explicitly track missing replies. Atlas does not simulate a response or force uniform agreement.
5. **Deliver:** Forge implements within scope; Atlas arranges the authorized executor and durable result. Roles without tools cannot claim execution. Record author, executor and publisher separately.
6. **Review/test:** Argus challenges relevant risks; Sentinel tests applicable acceptance criteria against exact version/environment. Required missing proof blocks overall acceptance. Route issues by category under Rulebook §5. One bounded correction round should target findings; escalate changed decisions instead of looping.
7. **Close:** Atlas reports actual outcome, evidence, limits, pending decisions and next owner. USER_GO, acceptance, release and merge remain separate. No claim of automatic future communication unless an actual authorized trigger exists.
8. **Learn when triggered:** Any member → Atlas → Cadence. Record finding/receipt/report → Peter+Atlas decision → authorized change → later effectiveness observation. No permanent poller or universal Cadence gate.

## Minimal reusable records
Task: `id | version | outcome | baseline | Epic/authority | relevant roles | critical assumption/proof | result/evidence | status | next owner`.
Finding: `category | observed evidence | impact/severity | smallest action | owner | verification | status`.
Process suggestion: `observation | evidence | affected roles/dissent | proposal or no-change | benefit/cost/delay | decision | effect check`.

Keep fields unknown rather than inventing data; omit irrelevant detail on small work. Use one source of task truth with links to distributed originals. Existing records are historical evidence, not current availability.

## Adoption check
Check all changed skill files and references; preserve Claude tool/model/effort frontmatter; review contradictions once; run small adversarial behavior probes. These checks validate the update, not the whole product. After integration, a real new invocation per Claude role must show which definition was loaded. Until then record `definition_updated_runtime_unverified`. No paid runtime/CI/relay activation is authorized by this document.

## Product-development integration
For material product work use `product-development-practice-v1.md`: user/problem and benefit evidence → goal and ordered next slice → cheapest valid test of the critical assumption → usable delivery meeting applicable DoD → product feedback and backlog adaptation. Reuse these steps within the loop above; do not create a parallel process. Atlas is Product Owner and coordinates delivery; Peter is customer and commissioning sponsor, the team plans and shares success/failure, and Cadence supplies the agile-coach/Scrum-Master function through an actual invocation. Product review and process retrospective serve different questions. A milestone or evidenced process failure may trigger Cadence; each story does not need a new process gate.
